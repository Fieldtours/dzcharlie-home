The story

As Nationalist forces overran Catalonia after the fall of Barcelona on 26 January 1939, a huge mass of civilians and defeated soldiers fled north to the French border: the Retirada. They crossed at Le Perthus/La Jonquera, Portbou, Coll d'Ares and the mountain passes, in winter, under air attack. France held many of them in improvised camps on the beaches such as Argelès and Saint-Cyprien. The MUME, opened in 2008 in the frontier town of La Jonquera, tells the story of this exodus and of the Republican exile: the camps, return, deportation to Nazi camps, and life abroad.

What you can see today

Permanent exhibition on the Retirada, the French camps and exile, with photographs, objects and testimonies; temporary exhibitions and a documentation centre.

Plan your visit

Carrer Major 43–47, La Jonquera; open Monday–Saturday and Sunday mornings; see museuexili.cat for hours and prices. The museum can advise on the exile walking routes over the nearby passes.
