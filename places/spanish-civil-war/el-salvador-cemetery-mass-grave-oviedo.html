The story

After Asturias fell in October 1937, Francoist courts-martial and extrajudicial killings claimed many lives. About 1,000 executions took place in Oviedo alone, and killings continued into the 1950s. Many of those shot against the walls of the municipal cemetery of El Salvador, at San Esteban de las Cruces, were buried in a common grave there. The names of 1,316 identified victims are now engraved on walls around the grave, making it one of the largest memorials of its kind in northern Spain. Earlier, in the Republican zone, rightists and clergy had also been killed in Asturias in 1934 and 1936.

What you can see today

Memorial walls engraved with the names of identified victims around the mass-grave area inside the cemetery.

Plan your visit

Open during cemetery hours; please be respectful. It is south of the city centre in San Esteban de las Cruces.
