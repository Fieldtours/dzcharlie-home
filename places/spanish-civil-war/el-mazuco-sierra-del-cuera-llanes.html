The story

After Santander fell, the Nationalists pushed west into Asturias. At the El Mazuco pass and on the limestone heights of the Sierra del Cuera, about 5,000 Republican troops of the XIV Army Corps, many of them CNT militia, held off Navarrese brigades under José Solchaga. Those brigades were far stronger, at about 33,000 men, and were supported by the Condor Legion, Italian units and naval gunfire. The Condor Legion used some of its first carpet-bombing tactics here, but the rugged terrain blunted their effect. Peña Turbina, the highest point of the range, was the last main Republican position; it fell on 22 September, opening eastern Asturias.

What you can see today

A small mountain village below rocky heights. Hiking routes such as Alto de la Tornería–Los Resquilones cross the former front line, where informal traces of positions remain.

Plan your visit

About 16 km south of Llanes on the AS-115. Stout boots are needed for the karst hillsides; the bar in El Mazucu is the only service.

Access
Official walk Alto de la Tornería–Pico de los Resquilones: low difficulty, about 1 h 15 min return
