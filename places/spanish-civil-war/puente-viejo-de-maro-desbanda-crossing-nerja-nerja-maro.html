The story

The old bridge over the Barranco de Maro, east of Nerja, carried the coast road used by the refugees fleeing Málaga in February 1937. Nearby the road was shelled from the sea, and at the Río de la Miel crossing many were killed. In Nerja itself, evacuation buses with children were bombed. The bridge was declared a Place of Democratic Memory in 2013.

What you can see today

The old stone bridge over the ravine on the old coast road near Maro, with a memorial plaque.

Plan your visit

Park in Maro and walk; about 3 km east of Nerja.

Where sources disagree: Numbers killed on this stretch are unknown.
