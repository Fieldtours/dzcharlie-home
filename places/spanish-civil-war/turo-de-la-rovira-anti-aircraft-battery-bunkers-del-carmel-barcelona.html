The story

The Republican anti-aircraft defence (DECA) built its main battery on this 262 m hill because it commanded the city. Four Vickers 105 mm guns, directed by acoustic locators and searchlights, fired on the Italian and German bombers raiding Barcelona in 1937–38. The guns were disabled as the Republicans withdrew on 25–26 January 1939, just before Nationalist troops entered the city. After the war the emplacements were taken over by shanty housing, which remained until 1990. In 2011 the Barcelona History Museum (MUHBA) restored the site as a place explaining both chapters of its history.

What you can see today

Concrete gun platforms, the command post and barrack foundations, shanty-town floor tiles, and a 360° view over Barcelona.

Plan your visit

Free, open-air site; steep walk up from Carrer Marià Labèrnia (buses V17/22/119 go close). Busy at sunset; the small MUHBA interpretation space has limited opening hours.
