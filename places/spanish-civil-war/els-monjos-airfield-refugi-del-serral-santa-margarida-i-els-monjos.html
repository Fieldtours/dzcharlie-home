The story

The Republican air force had a military airfield near Els Monjos in the Penedès, part of a network of Catalan fields that sheltered fighters and bombers from 1937. Because the airfield made the area a bombing target, a shelter for about 120 people was dug in 1937 next to the Serral farmhouse, which was used as a barracks. Its brick-vaulted galleries and three ventilation shafts survive and can be visited as part of the municipality's airfield memory route. It is a rare surviving piece of the Republic's wartime air infrastructure in the Penedès.

What you can see today

A brick barrel-vaulted shelter with ventilation shafts beside the Serral farmhouse; remains of the airfield landscape around.

Plan your visit

The shelter is visited with CIARGA (Centre d'Interpretació de l'Aviació Republicana i la Guerra Aèria), Parc de la Memòria, Santa Margarida i els Monjos. It usually opens at weekends and on holidays, 10:00–14:00, for a small fee; check with the town before you go.
