The story

In 1936 Gernika dug four tunnel shelters into the hillside by the market area to protect townspeople from air raids. They were in use on 26 April 1937, when crowds from the Monday market and refugees sheltered here as the bombs fell. Two of the four shelters survive. One unfinished tunnel holds a scale model of the shelter as built; the other has a sound-and-light presentation recreating the experience of the people inside during the raid. It is a short, powerful complement to the Peace Museum.

What you can see today

Two restored tunnel shelters about 10 m into the hillside, with a model and an immersive audiovisual.

Plan your visit

Entrance on Juan Calzada Kalea, near the Pasealeku park. Free entry; check times with the Gernika tourist office (turismo.gernika-lumo.eus).
