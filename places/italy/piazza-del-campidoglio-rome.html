The story

On 5 June 1944, the morning after American troops entered Rome, General Mark Clark held his famous press conference on the Capitoline Hill. For a day Rome was the world's headline, until the Normandy landings on 6 June pushed Italy off the front pages, where it largely stayed.

What to look for

Stand at the top of the Cordonata staircase and look over the city that was liberated almost undamaged.

Plan your visit

Open square; the Capitoline Museums are separate and ticketed.
