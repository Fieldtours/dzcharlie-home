The story

The vast Bourbon palace became an Allied headquarters in the autumn of 1943, first for the US Fifth Army and later for Allied Force Headquarters. Here, on 29 April 1945, German envoys signed the surrender of German and Italian Fascist forces in Italy, which took effect on 2 May. The campaign you are following ended in these rooms.

What to look for

The royal apartments, the grand staircase and the long park with its cascade; imagine offices, map rooms and soldiers' rest camps in the grounds.
