The story

About 20,000 German soldiers who died in southern Italy lie on the terraced slope of Colle Marino, gathered here from many field graves in 1959–60. The paratroopers who held Cassino are among them. It is a sober counterpart to the Allied cemeteries in the valley.

What to look for

Climb to the bronze cross at the top for a view back down the Rapido valley to Cassino and the abbey.
