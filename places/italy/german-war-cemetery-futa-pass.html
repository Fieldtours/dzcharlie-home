The story

Italy's largest German war cemetery holds more than 30,600 dead, gathered from across the region, in terraces spiralling up a hilltop on the old Gothic Line. Designed by Dieter Oesterlen and dedicated in 1969, its 2 km stone wall climbs to a sharp prow-like summit. The graves are of the defenders of the Line, and of men from every Italian front after Rome.

What to look for

Follow the spiral wall to the top for the view over the Futa and Giogo passes; note how young most of the men were.

Plan your visit

Via San Jacopo a Castro, Traversa (Firenzuola), on the SS65 at the Futa Pass. Open daily, free; hours vary by season — check before you go.
