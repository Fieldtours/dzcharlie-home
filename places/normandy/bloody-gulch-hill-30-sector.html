The story

On 13 June German forces counterattacked south-west of Carentan. The 506th held until armour arrived. For Easy a test of holding ground, not seizing it. The exact hedgerow line is disputed.

What to look for

Battlefield terrain, not a single-point attraction: hedgerows, folds and restricted sightlines.
