Spanish Civil War 1936–1939

Spanish Civil War sites in the Basque Country & the North

In 1937 the Nationalists conquered the Republican north. On 26 April Gernika was bombed by aircraft of the German Condor Legion and the Italian air force, an attack remembered around the world through Picasso's painting.

Bilbao, defended by the line of bunkers and trenches called the Iron Belt (Cinturón de Hierro), fell in June 1937; Santander followed in August and Asturias in October. Below are the places from our free Spanish Civil War guide in the Basque Country, Cantabria and Asturias, told fairly from both sides.

Artxanda – Iron Belt trenches & memorial

Trenches & fortifications · 12–19 Jun 1937 · allow 1 h

Mount Artxanda, above Bilbao, was the last defended position before the city fell.

Astra factory air-raid shelter, Gernika

Air-raid shelter · Sep 1936–Apr 1937 · allow 20 min

Gernika's arms industry, which made the famous Astra pistols, was one of the few potentially military targets in the town. In September 1936 the Talleres de Gernika factory built two concrete shelters for its workers.

Casa de Juntas & Tree of Gernika

City site · 26 Apr 1937 · allow 45 min

The Assembly House (Casa de Juntas) and the oak of Gernika are the historic symbols of Biscayan and Basque self-government: the Lords of Biscay swore under the tree to respect local liberties.

Ceares cemetery mass grave, Gijón

Repression & memory site · 1937–1950s · allow 30 min

After Gijón fell on 21 October 1937, thousands of Republicans were imprisoned and many were tried by courts-martial and shot. Victims executed at the wall of the Ceares municipal cemetery were buried in a common grave there.

Durango bombing – Santa María de Uribarri

Memorial · 31 Mar 1937 · allow 1 h

On 31 March 1937, the first day of General Mola's offensive against Biscay, Italian Savoia-Marchetti S.81 bombers with German and Italian escorts attacked Durango, a road and rail junction behind the front.

El Salvador cemetery mass grave, Oviedo

Repression & memory site · 1937–1950s · allow 30 min

After Asturias fell in October 1937, Francoist courts-martial and extrajudicial killings claimed many lives. About 1,000 executions took place in Oviedo alone, and killings continued into the 1950s.

Gaztelumendi breach – Larrabetzu Iron Belt bunker

Trenches & fortifications · 12 Jun 1937 · allow 45 min

The Iron Belt was built from late 1936 as a ring of trenches, bunkers and gun positions around Bilbao.

Gernika Peace Museum (Museo de la Paz)

Museum · 26 Apr 1937 · allow 1 h 15 min

On the afternoon of Monday 26 April 1937 – market day – aircraft of the German Condor Legion, with Italian Aviazione Legionaria bombers, attacked Gernika for about three hours in support of the Nationalist advance on Bilbao.

Intxorta front & Elgeta memory centre

Interpretation centre · Sep 1936–Apr 1937 · allow 3 h

After the loss of most of Gipuzkoa in 1936, Basque and Republican battalions stabilised the front on the Intxorta heights above Elgeta.

Iron Belt Interpretation Centre, Berango

Interpretation centre · 1936–Jun 1937 · allow 1 h 30 min

The Iron Belt's western end ran through the Uribe district north of Bilbao to the coast at Barrika and Sopela.

Iron Belt trail, Zamudio (Berreaga–Gaztelumendi ridge)

Trenches & fortifications · 11–13 Jun 1937 · allow 4 h

Zamudio lies directly below the stretch of the Iron Belt where the Nationalists broke through.

Otxandio – Andikona bombing memorial

Memorial · 22 Jul 1936 · allow 30 min

On 22 July 1936, four days after the coup, two Breguet XIX light bombers flown from Recajo (La Rioja) by Ángel Salas Larrazábal and José Muñoz Jiménez bombed and machine-gunned the Andikona square in Otxandio.

Oviedo front – Cerdeño bunker

Trenches & fortifications · Oct 1936–Oct 1937 · allow 30 min

Once the Galician relief column had reached Oviedo in October 1936, the Nationalist defenders ringed the city with concrete positions facing the Republican lines on Monte Naranco and in the surrounding villages.

Private land

Oviedo siege – the old town

City site · 19 Jul 1936–Feb 1937 · allow 30 min

On 19 July 1936 Colonel Antonio Aranda, who had persuaded Republican leaders he was loyal, declared for the rising and held Oviedo with soldiers, Civil Guards and Falangist volunteers.

Pasealekua air-raid shelter, Gernika

Air-raid shelter · 1936–26 Apr 1937 · allow 30 min

In 1936 Gernika dug four tunnel shelters into the hillside by the market area to protect townspeople from air raids.

Puerto del Escudo – Italian memorial

Memorial · 14–17 Aug 1937 · allow 30 min

The Escudo pass (about 1,000 m) carries the main road from Burgos to Santander over the Cantabrian range. When the Nationalist offensive on Santander opened on 14 August 1937, Italian CTV divisions attacked here.

San Marcial hermitage & the Battle of Irun

Battlefield · 19 Aug–5 Sep 1936 · allow 1 h

Mola's Navarrese columns under Colonel Alfonso Beorlegui attacked Irun to cut the Republican north off from France. Their attack was supported by German and Italian aircraft and a few light tanks.

Visiting info checked

Santander air-raid shelter (Refugio Antiaéreo)

Air-raid shelter · 1937 · allow 40 min

Santander, then in the Republican zone, was bombed repeatedly during 1937, and over a hundred shelters were dug beneath the city. This one, under the Plaza del Príncipe (then Plaza de Mariana Pineda), was built in spring 1937.

Booking neededVisiting info checked

Simancas barracks siege memorial, Gijón

City site · 19 Jul–16 Aug 1936 · allow 20 min

When the coup failed in Gijón, Colonel Antonio Pinilla and about 600 soldiers of the Simancas mountain infantry regiment shut themselves into the Simancas barracks, a former Jesuit college.
