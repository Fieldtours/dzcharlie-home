Spanish Civil War 1936–1939

Spanish Civil War sites in Madrid

In November 1936 Franco's forces reached the edge of Madrid. The attack was stopped in the Casa de Campo and the Ciudad Universitaria, and the front stayed there for the rest of the war while the city lived under siege and bombing. Madrid only fell at the end of March 1939.

Around the capital came some of the war's biggest battles: the Jarama in February 1937, Guadalajara in March and Brunete in July. Below are the places from our free Spanish Civil War guide in Madrid and the centre of Spain, told fairly from both sides.

Madrid & the Centre

Alcázar of Toledo & Army Museum

Museum · 21 Jul–27 Sep 1936 · allow 2 h 30 min

When the coup failed in Toledo, Colonel José Moscardó shut himself in the Alcázar with Civil Guards, cadets, Falangists and their families, plus a number of left-wing hostages.

Visiting info checked

Alto del León (south side), Guadarrama

Trenches & fortifications · 22 Jul 1936–Mar 1939 · allow 2 h 30 min

The Guadarrama pass, renamed Alto de los Leones by the victors, was one of the first battlefields of the war.

Arco de la Victoria (Moncloa)

Memorial · Built 1950–1956 · allow 15 min

This 40 m triumphal arch at the entrance to the University City was built in 1950–56 to commemorate the Francoist victory in the battle for the University City, where the front ran from November 1936 to March 1939.

Blockhaus 13, Colmenar del Arroyo

Brihuega & Palacio de Ibarra

Battlefield · 8–23 Mar 1937 · allow 1 h 30 min

In March 1937 Mussolini's Corpo Truppe Volontarie under General Roatta drove down the Zaragoza road towards Guadalajara to encircle Madrid from the north-east.

Brunete

Ruined village · 6–25 Jul 1937 · allow 45 min

On 6 July 1937 the Republic launched its first great offensive west of Madrid, aiming to cut off the Nationalist troops besieging the capital and to relieve pressure on the northern front.

Cerro Garabitas, Casa de Campo

Battlefield · 13 Nov 1936–Mar 1939 · allow 1 h 15 min

At 677 m, Garabitas is one of the highest points of the Casa de Campo, the former royal hunting estate through which Nationalist columns attacked Madrid in November 1936.

Ciudad Universitaria front

Battlefield · 15 Nov 1936–28 Mar 1939 · allow 1 h 30 min

In mid-November 1936 Nationalist columns under Varela and Asensio, spearheaded by Moroccan Regulares and the Legion, crossed the Manzanares and broke into the half-built University City.

Frente del Agua, Paredes de Buitrago

Trenches & fortifications · Jul 1936–Mar 1939 · allow 3 h

In late July 1936 rebel troops under Colonel García Escámez crossed the Somosierra and seized Paredes de Buitrago, close to the El Villar and Puentes Viejas reservoirs that supplied Madrid with water.

Fresnedillas de la Oliva bunkers

Trenches & fortifications · Nov 1936–1939 · allow 1 h

Nationalist troops took Fresnedillas de la Oliva on 5 November 1936 during the advance on Madrid; the front then halted on the Perales river and saw heavy fighting in December 1936.

Along the Morata–San Martín de la Vega road, near the XV Brigade's old kitchens at the Venta de Frascuelo, stand memorials to the British, Irish, American, Balkan and Franco-Belgian volunteers who fought on the Jarama.
